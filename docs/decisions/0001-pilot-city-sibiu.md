# 0001. Pilot city: Sibiu, launched through 3 commuting corridors

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Area:** Business
- **Agent:** product-strategist

## Context
A carpool marketplace lives or dies on liquidity, meaning enough drivers and passengers on the same path at the same time. The brief proposes Sibiu, the owner's city. The infrastructure budget is near zero, so growth must come from local, organic seeding rather than paid acquisition.

## Options considered
| Option | Pros | Cons | Cost / effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Sibiu, whole city, no focus | Simple message | Demand spread thin, so empty searches early | Low |
| B. Sibiu, citywide app with marketing focused on 3 corridors (South: Cisnădie/Șelimbăr to centre; West: to Zona Industrială Vest; Campus: to ULBS) | Density where commutes converge. The owner can seed locally. Compact 4–12 km trips | Corridors outside the focus are weaker at first | Low |
| C. Cluj-Napoca | About 3 times larger population, more students | No founder presence, more dispersed hubs, heavier Bolt/Uber presence | Medium–high |

## Decision
Option B, **Sibiu with 3 launch corridors**. The founder's local presence and Sibiu's compact, hub-centred commuting give the best odds of reaching liquidity on a zero budget. Expand to a second city only after ≥ 300 completed shared seats per week for 4 consecutive weeks.

## Consequences
- Positive: focused recruiting (employers, ULBS, local groups). Short trips keep prices at 1–4 RON.
- Negative / accepted risks: small total market (134k city + suburbs). Results may not transfer to larger cities.
- What would trigger revisiting: match rate < 15% in all corridors at week 12, or a strong partner (employer or municipality) in another city.
