# 0006. Brand name: Haide

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Area:** Brand
- **Agent:** brand-designer

## Context
The product needs a name before logo, store listings and domain work can start. Criteria: two syllables ideal, easy to spell after hearing it, works in English, a positive or neutral meaning in Romanian (pilot city is Sibiu), and not confusingly similar to an existing mobility brand. The positioning is "the neighbourly way across Sibiu" ([strategy §3](../product/strategy.md)), so the name should sound like an invitation, not a platform. Twelve candidates were explored and four shortlisted; details and checks are in [brand-guide §2](../brand/brand-guide.md).

## Options considered
| Option | Pros | Cons | Cost / effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. **Haide** ("come on, let's go") | Instantly Romanian and warm, the exact moment a shared ride starts, 2 syllables, no mobility product with this name found. Near-neighbours are not mobility (haidihai.ro, HAID fitness app, a `haide.io` template repo) | English speakers may say "hayd" (fixed with a pronunciation line). Meaning is lost on non-Romanian speakers, which does not matter for a Sibiu pilot. Domain and trademark not verified | Low |
| B. Hopa | Playful, 2 syllables, "hop in" feel | Exclamation rather than invitation, no link to sharing, also a town in Turkey | Low |
| C. Tandem | Perfect English metaphor for two riding together | Meaningless in Romanian. Collides with the Tandem language-exchange app (10M+ downloads), Tandem Mobility bike-share and RideTandem | Low, but high collision risk |
| D. Tura | Short, Romanian word for a round or shift | No clear meaning for sharing, reads as a surname or a place name | Low |

## Decision
We choose **Haide**, pronounced "HIGH-deh", because it is the invitation that starts every shared ride, it is local to Sibiu, and the availability searches found no mobility product with the name. It is written **Haide** in text and `haide` (lowercase) in the wordmark.

## Consequences
- Positive: memorable, short, strong local resonance, supports the neighbourly positioning, easy to turn into copy ("Haide, let's go").
- Negative / accepted risks: only web searches were possible, so this is **not** legal clearance. Domain (`haide.ro` first choice), store-name availability and an OSIM/EUIPO knock-out search are owner-side tasks listed in [brand-guide §11](../brand/brand-guide.md). Pronunciation guidance is needed for non-Romanian speakers.
- What would trigger revisiting this decision: a trademark conflict in classes 9, 39 or 42, no usable domain or store name, or a decision to expand to a market where the word is confusing. Fallback candidate: Hopa.
