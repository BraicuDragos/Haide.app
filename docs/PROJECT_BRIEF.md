# Project brief — city carpooling app

> Starting document from the owner. The agent team expands it in `docs/product/`.

## Vision
A "BlaBlaCar for the city": trips **inside a city** (commuting, going to university/work,
errands), not long-distance trips between cities.

## Core mechanics
1. **A driver publishes a route**: from where, to where, when, how many free seats.
2. **Passengers search and join** routes that suit them (path + time).
3. **The price is very low**: based on **cost-sharing**, not profit.

## Constraints and preferences
- Pilot city: **Sibiu, Romania** (the owner's city). The strategist may argue for a different choice.
- The first version must be a working MVP (Minimum Viable Product), easy to use from a phone.
- **Language: English** for the app UI and all project artifacts.
- The owner wants minimal involvement: the team makes the business, brand, UX and architecture
  decisions and documents them.
- Infrastructure budget: as close to zero as possible (free tiers) for the MVP.

## Open (the team decides)
Name and brand, platform (web/PWA/native mobile), stack, monetization model,
exact pricing formula, trust & safety mechanisms, route matching.
